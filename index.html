A day at the counter

Every customer brings a story and a price.

  1. 01

    Someone walks in

    A trumpet from a grandfather's parade band, a gold pocket watch, a motorcycle on a dolly. Sellers, buyers and people who need a loan come through the door all day.

  2. 02

    Look closer

    Turn it over and read the card. Weigh the gold and test it with acid, plug in what plugs in, and call the expert when you're not sure. Some things are worth far more than they look. Some are worth far less.

  3. 03

    Haggle on the register

    Dial in your offer and watch their face. Go too low and they walk. Check ClutterBid for what the same thing really sold for.

  4. 04

    Price it and put it out

    Tag it, put it in the case, and sell it to the next customer who wants it. Or hold it as a pawn and hope they come back to pay.

  5. 05

    Close up and count the drawer

    List the slow sellers online, take the best offer in the morning, and spend the profit on the next part of the shop.
