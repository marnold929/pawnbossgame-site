Pawn Boss — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 (first published October 7, 2026)

Pawn Boss is a single-player game for Meta Quest, published by Magnet Mates LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, processes and stores about you, what it is used for, and how to have it deleted.

Summary

The game collects and processes two kinds of data from your Meta account, through Meta's Platform SDK: your Meta user ID and your Meta user profile (your username, display name and profile picture). They are used only to verify your purchase, record your achievements, and show your name on the game's optional World Rankings leaderboards. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than Meta. Everything else the game stores stays on your headset.

The game sends this data only to Meta, using Meta's own SDK, over Meta's connection. We have no servers of our own, no accounts of our own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not receive a copy of any of the data above. We do not collect your email address, location, voice, camera images, or hand or body tracking data.

Data that stays on your headset

Your saved games, your settings and a short technical log used for troubleshooting are stored in the game's own storage on your headset. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you press PHOTO in the game, a picture of your in-game medallion board is saved to your headset's Pictures folder ("Pictures/Pawn Boss"). It stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time from the headset's Files app.

Hand tracking and controller tracking are used only to let you play. That data is processed on the headset by the Meta Quest system and is never stored or transmitted by the game.

How long data is kept

Your user ID and profile are held by the game only while it is running. Achievements and leaderboard scores are kept by Meta until you or Meta delete them (see below). Data on your headset is kept until you delete it or uninstall the game.

How to delete your data

You can request deletion of any data the game has created about you in either of these ways:

  • Email us. Send a request to hello@magnetmates.app with the subject "Pawn Boss data deletion" and the Meta username you play under. We will confirm within 7 days, ask Meta to remove your Pawn Boss achievements and leaderboard entries, and finish within 30 days. We will also delete any copy of your request once it is done.
  • Through Meta. Because achievements and leaderboard scores are stored by Meta, you can also manage or remove them using the Meta Horizon app or Meta's account tools, including by deleting the app's data or your Meta account. Meta's handling of your data is described in Meta's privacy policy.

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your headset. It does not by itself delete achievements or scores held by Meta; use one of the two options above for those.

Children

Pawn Boss is not directed at children under 13. The game does not knowingly collect data from children, and the Meta account features it uses are provided by Meta under the age rules Meta applies to that account. If you believe a child's data has been recorded through the game, email us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We share no data with anyone. The only party that receives data from the game is Meta, through its own platform, as described above. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising.

Changes

If a future update adds another online feature or collects anything new, this policy will be updated before that feature is released, and the game will describe the feature before you use it.
