Pawn Boss — Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026 (first published October 7, 2026)

Pawn Boss is a single-player game for Meta Quest, published by Magnet Mates LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. The only data the game touches outside your headset is handled by Meta's own platform, for achievements and leaderboards, as described below.

What we collect

Nothing on our side. The game has no accounts of its own, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We have no servers. We do not receive your name, email address, Meta account details, location, voice, camera images, hand or body tracking data, or anything about how you play.

What stays on your headset

Your saved games, settings, and a short technical log used for troubleshooting are stored in the game's own storage on your headset. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you press PHOTO in the game, a picture of your in-game medallion board is saved to your headset's Pictures folder ("Pictures/Pawn Boss"). It stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time from the headset's Files app.

Hand tracking and controller tracking are used only to let you play. That data is processed on the headset by the Meta Quest system and is never stored or transmitted by the game.

Meta platform services

The game uses Meta's Platform SDK for three things. All of them are carried out by Meta on Meta's systems; we do not run any servers and do not receive copies of this data.

  • Purchase check. When the game starts, it confirms that the game was purchased on the account in use.
  • Achievements. When you reach an in-game milestone (for example, your first sale or buying the building), the game tells Meta to record that achievement against your Meta account, using your Meta user ID, so it persists if you reinstall and can appear on your Meta profile. Meta stores and displays achievements under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • World Rankings leaderboards (when enabled). The game may submit your shop's scores (best day, biggest find, weekly profit) to Meta's leaderboard service under your Meta user ID, and the in-game rankings board may show your Meta username and profile photo next to your score, along with other players' usernames and scores as Meta provides them. Scores are stored by Meta, not by us.

To see or remove achievement and leaderboard data, use the Meta Horizon app or Meta's account tools, since that data lives with Meta. You can also stop the game from writing any of it by uninstalling the game. Meta's own handling of your data when you use a Quest headset and the Meta Horizon Store is described in Meta's privacy policies.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children. Achievements and leaderboards are provided by Meta under the account rules Meta applies to that account.

Deletion

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete: uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your headset, and achievement or leaderboard data is managed through your Meta account. If you believe we hold any information about you, email us and we will confirm and delete it.

Changes

If a future update adds another online feature, this policy will be updated before that feature is released, and the game will describe the feature before you use it.
