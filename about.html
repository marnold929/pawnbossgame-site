A small business, start to finish

Magnet Mates: photo magnets, pressed by hand, ordered from a phone.

The problem

Every order starts with a customer's photos, and each photo has to be cropped to the exact shape of the magnet before it prints. The business needed a way for customers to do that themselves, pay, and follow their order.

What we built

An online store plus iPhone and Android apps. Customers pick a style, upload full-quality photos and crop each one before checkout. The original is kept and a high-resolution print crop is made from it. Businesses can send a logo and ask for samples and bulk pricing.

What changed

Customers place, track and reorder from their phone. The app is rated 5.0 on the App Store. It started on a hosted app builder, and when the running costs and security stopped making sense we moved it to our own setup.
